Everything below this tab is the detailed, analyst-level breakdown — cohort tables, retention triangles, methodology notes — behind the same numbers shown above.
Attempt-week cohort (2026-08-21 rework). Each row is a real lead attempt, not a contact — bucketed by the week that specific attempt was created. A contact who tries, fails, and retries weeks later shows up in both weeks; the attempt that actually converts gets the credit in its own week, not retroactively folded into the first one. Once a contact has ever Won (reached Activated), every later lead they generate is a reactivation and is excluded here entirely — see the note above and docs/Funnel.md for that as its own dimension. Because reached_installed ⇒ reached_lead, reached_kyc_done ⇒ reached_installed, etc. hold per attempt, each column is ≤ the one before it — nothing here can read over 100% or increase step to step.
Grain: every count on this tab is attempt-grain (flow — cohorts, conversion). Population/stock counts (how many people) are contact-grain and live on the Summary tab headline — never compare the two directly (27,492 attempts ≠ 26,438 people).
